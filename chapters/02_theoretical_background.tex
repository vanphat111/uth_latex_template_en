\section{Mathematical Formulation}
\label{sec:math}

[Explain equations and mathematical derivations using amsmath.]

A sample numbered equation:
\begin{equation}
    f(x) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \cdot x_i + b
    \label{eq:sample_linear}
\end{equation}
where $w_i$ represents the weight coefficients, $x_i$ denotes the input variables, and $b$ is the bias term.

Multi-line aligned mathematical derivation:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
    \mathcal{L}(\theta) &= -\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left[ y_i \log(\hat{y}_i) + (1 - y_i) \log(1 - \hat{y}_i) \right] \\
    &= \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \ell_i(\theta)
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:sample_loss}
\end{equation}

As defined in Equation~\eqref{eq:sample_linear} and Equation~\eqref{eq:sample_loss}, the parameters are optimized iteratively.
